The Artist

About Tanmay Sarkar

Tanmay Sarkar is a visual artist working across painting, sketching and mixed media, with a practice built on patient line work, colour and close observation. Every piece on this page began as a small idea worked out slowly, on paper and canvas, over many quiet sittings.

Alongside a personal studio practice, Tanmay runs Rainbow Colour Art School, teaching students of all ages to draw, paint and see the world in colour — the results of which are celebrated every year in THE TALENT SHOWCASE.
